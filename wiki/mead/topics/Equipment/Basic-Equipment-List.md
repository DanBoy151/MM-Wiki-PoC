## Summary

What you need to make mead.

## Details

**Starter kit**: a fermenter with airlock (a carboy or bucket), sanitizer, a hydrometer or refractometer, an auto-siphon, a scale accurate enough for small nutrient doses, and bottles or a keg setup. No boil kettle strictly required, though many brewers heat the must briefly to help dissolve honey and pasteurize.

**Nice-to-haves**: a pH meter, extra carboys/vessels for long-term bulk aging (mead often needs more aging capacity than beer since it sits longer), and accurate small-scale measuring for nutrient additions.
