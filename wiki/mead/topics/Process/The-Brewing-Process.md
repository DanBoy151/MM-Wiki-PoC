## Summary

The stages of making mead, end to end.

## Details

1. **Mix the must** — dissolve honey into water to hit your target starting gravity (`OG`); warming the water helps the honey dissolve.
2. **Pitch & nutrient schedule** — pitch yeast, then add nutrient in stages over the first third to half of active fermentation rather than all at once.
3. **Ferment** — primary fermentation often runs 2-4+ weeks depending on target `ABV` and yeast strain.
4. **Rack & clarify** — rack off the lees once gravity stabilizes; mead can take longer to clear than beer or cider.
5. **Bulk age** — condition for months (sometimes a year or more for stronger meads); this is where harsh alcohol notes fade and flavor rounds out.
6. **Package** — still, bottle-conditioned (sparkling), or force-carbonated in a keg.

Record the batch using [[../../../housekeeping/Templates/Recipe-Template|Recipe Template]] — list honey/adjuncts in the Fermentables table and nutrient additions in Misc / Water Agents.
