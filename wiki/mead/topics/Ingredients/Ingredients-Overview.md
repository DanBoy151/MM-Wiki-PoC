## Summary

The core ingredients in mead, and what each contributes.

## Details

**Honey** is the entire fermentable base — varietal choice (clover, wildflower, orange blossom, buckwheat, etc.) has a bigger flavor impact than most beginners expect. **Water** dilutes the honey to the target starting gravity. **Yeast** converts sugar to alcohol; mead-tolerant strains (e.g. 71B, D47, EC-1118) vary a lot in how much honey character they preserve versus ferment away, and in alcohol tolerance. **Nutrients** (e.g. Fermaid O/K, DAP) are close to essential — honey must is nutrient-poor and underfed fermentations are the most common source of off-flavors. **Adjuncts** define the mead's category: fruit (melomel), spices (metheglin), apple juice (cyser), grape juice (pyment), or malt (braggot).
