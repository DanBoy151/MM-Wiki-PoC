## Summary

What to expect making your first batch of mead.

## Details

Mead is honey, water, and yeast — simple in concept, but honey is nutrient-poor, so proper yeast nutrient additions matter far more here than in beer or cider. A staggered nutrient addition schedule (adding nutrient in stages early in fermentation, e.g. a TOSNA-style schedule) makes a big difference to fermentation health and off-flavor risk. Expect a longer timeline than beer: primary fermentation can take a few weeks, and mead often needs months (sometimes longer) of conditioning to taste its best — it rewards patience.
