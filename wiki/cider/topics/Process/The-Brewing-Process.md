## Summary

The stages of making cider, end to end.

## Details

1. **Source juice** — fresh-pressed or store-bought (preservative-free), check/adjust starting gravity if you want a specific target `ABV`.
2. **Sanitize & pitch** — sanitize the fermenter, pitch yeast (and nutrient, if using), and seal with an airlock.
3. **Ferment** — primary fermentation typically runs 1-3 weeks; cider often benefits from a slower, cooler fermentation than beer.
4. **Rack & clarify** — rack off the sediment (lees) once fermentation slows, optionally using pectic enzyme or a fining agent for clarity.
5. **Condition** — bulk age for weeks to months; this is where a lot of cider's character develops.
6. **Package** — still, bottle-conditioned (sparkling), or force-carbonated in a keg.
