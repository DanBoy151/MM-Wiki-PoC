## Summary

Common cider off-flavors, their usual causes, and what to check next time.

## Details

- **Thin/watery body** — juice lacking tannin or body; consider a tannin addition or a blend with bittersweet apples next time.
- **Excess acidity/tartness** — juice too sharp; can mellow with age, or be blended/back-sweetened to balance.
- **Vinegar/sour** — acetobacter infection, usually from too much oxygen exposure; minimize headspace and splashing.
- **Rotten egg (sulfur)** — stressed or nutrient-starved yeast; usually fades with conditioning, but add nutrient earlier next time.
- **Cidery/solvent harshness** — high fermentation temperature; ferment cooler for a cleaner result.
- **Excess sweetness/stuck fermentation** — high-sugar juice or a yeast that can't finish; check `OG`/`FG` against the yeast's expected attenuation.
