## Summary

What to expect making your first batch of cider.

## Details

Cider is one of the simplest fermented beverages to start with: fresh or store-bought juice, yeast, and sanitized equipment. Use juice with no preservatives (check the label for potassium sorbate or sodium benzoate — either will stop fermentation). Take a starting gravity reading (`OG`) before pitching yeast so you know your potential `ABV`, and be patient — cider often benefits from a longer, cooler fermentation and a few weeks to a few months of conditioning before it tastes its best.
