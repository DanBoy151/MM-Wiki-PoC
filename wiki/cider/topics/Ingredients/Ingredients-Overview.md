## Summary

The core ingredients in cider, and what each contributes.

## Details

**Apple juice** provides the fermentable sugar plus the acid, tannin, and aromatic character that define the finished cider — juice blends (sharp, bittersweet, sweet apple varieties) make a real difference. **Yeast** converts the sugar to alcohol and shapes aroma; cider-specific or wine yeasts are common, though ale yeast works too. **Nutrients** (e.g. Fermaid) matter more in cider than beer since juice is often nutrient-poor, and a stressed fermentation is a common source of off-flavors. **Additions** like pectic enzyme (clarity), tannin, or acid blend are used to correct thin or unbalanced juice.
