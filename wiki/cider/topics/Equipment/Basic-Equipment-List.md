## Summary

What you need to make cider, from a first batch to pressing your own apples.

## Details

**Juice-based starter kit**: a fermenter with airlock, sanitizer, a hydrometer or refractometer, an auto-siphon, and bottles or a keg setup. No boil kettle needed unless you're pasteurizing fresh-pressed juice.

**If pressing your own apples**: an apple grinder/scratter, a press, and food-grade buckets for collecting juice, plus a way to pasteurize if the juice won't be fermented immediately.

**Nice-to-haves**: a pH meter and acid titration kit (cider benefits a lot from balanced acidity), and temperature-controlled fermentation for cleaner flavor.
