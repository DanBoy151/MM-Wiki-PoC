## Summary

What to expect making your first batch of wine.

## Details

Start with a kit (concentrate/juice bucket) or fresh juice rather than whole grapes — it removes the crush/press step and most of the acid/sugar guesswork while you learn the fermentation and racking side. Sulfite (Campden tablets or potassium metabisulfite) management matters more in wine than beer: it protects against oxidation and spoilage organisms at several points (must, racking, bottling). Expect a much longer timeline than beer — wine typically wants months of bulk aging, and sometimes bottle aging beyond that, before it's at its best.
