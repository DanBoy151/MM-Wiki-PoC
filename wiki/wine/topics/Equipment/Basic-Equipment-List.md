## Summary

What you need to make wine, from a first kit to working with fresh grapes.

## Details

**Kit/juice starter kit**: a primary fermenter (bucket with lid), a glass or PET carboy for secondary/bulk aging, an airlock and bung, sanitizer, a hydrometer, an auto-siphon, and a corker/bottles or a keg setup.

**If working with fresh grapes**: a crusher/destemmer, a press, food-grade fermentation vessels sized for must (skins and all) during primary, and extra carboys for the longer aging period wine needs.

**Nice-to-haves**: a pH meter and acid titration kit (acid balance matters a lot in wine), a wine thief for sampling, and a sulfite testing kit (or at least a consistent dosing schedule) to manage oxidation.
