## Summary

Common wine off-flavors, their usual causes, and what to check next time.

## Details

- **Oxidation (browning, sherry-like, flat fruit)** — insufficient sulfite dosing or too much headspace/time between top-ups; keep vessels topped up and maintain a sulfite schedule.
- **Vinegar/sour (acetic)** — acetobacter infection from oxygen exposure; minimize splashing and headspace.
- **Rotten egg (sulfur/H2S)** — nutrient-starved or stressed fermentation; consider a nutrient addition on the next batch.
- **Excess bite/harshness** — high tannin or acid; can mellow with extended aging, or correct the must next time.
- **Stuck fermentation** — high starting gravity outrunning yeast tolerance, temperature swings, or sulfite added too soon after pitching.
- **Cloudiness that won't clear** — pectin haze (needs pectic enzyme) or protein/microbial haze (may need fining).
