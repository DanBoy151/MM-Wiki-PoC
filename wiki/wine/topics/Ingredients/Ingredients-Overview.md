## Summary

The core ingredients in wine, and what each contributes.

## Details

**Grapes/juice/concentrate** provide the fermentable sugar along with the acid, tannin, and varietal character that define the wine. **Yeast** converts sugar to alcohol; wine yeast strains (e.g. EC-1118, D47, RC212) vary in alcohol tolerance and how much they emphasize fruit versus ferment it away. **Sulfites** (potassium metabisulfite/Campden) protect against oxidation and unwanted microbes at multiple stages. **Adjuncts** like acid blend, tannin powder, and pectic enzyme correct thin, flat, or hazy musts, and oak (chips, spirals, barrel) can add complexity during aging.
