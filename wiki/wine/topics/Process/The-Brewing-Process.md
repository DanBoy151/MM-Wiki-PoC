## Summary

The stages of making wine, end to end.

## Details

1. **Prepare the must** — crush/press fresh grapes, or reconstitute juice/concentrate; check and adjust starting gravity (`OG`) and acidity.
2. **Sulfite & settle** — dose with sulfite to control wild microbes and oxidation before pitching a chosen yeast strain.
3. **Ferment** — primary fermentation (on the skins for reds) typically runs 1-2 weeks; press off skins, then continue secondary fermentation to dryness.
4. **Rack & clarify** — rack off the lees periodically over weeks to months; fine and/or filter if needed for clarity.
5. **Bulk age** — condition for months, topping up vessels to minimize headspace/oxidation; malolactic fermentation may happen here for some styles.
6. **Bottle** — bottle once stable and clear; many wines continue improving with bottle age.

Record the batch using [[../../../housekeeping/Templates/Recipe-Template|Recipe Template]] — list grapes/juice in the Fermentables table and sulfite/acid/tannin additions in Misc / Water Agents.
