## Summary

What to expect brewing your first batch of beer, and the handful of things that actually matter early on.

## Details

Sanitation matters more than technique. A clean, cool, low-stress fermentation will save a mediocre recipe; a dirty fermenter will ruin a great one. Beyond that: pick a simple, forgiving style (see [[../../bjcp-styles/1-style-standard-american-beer/American-Wheat-Beer|American Wheat Beer]] or an ale in the [[../../bjcp-styles/18-style-pale-american-ale/Blonde-Ale|Pale American Ale]] category), use an extract or partial-mash kit for your first attempt, and take a hydrometer/gravity reading at the start (`OG`) and once fermentation looks done (`FG`) — see [[../../../housekeeping/Glossary|Glossary]] for what those mean. Don't rush bottling; an extra week in the fermenter rarely hurts.
