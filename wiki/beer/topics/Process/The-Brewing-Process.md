## Summary

The stages of an all-grain brew day, end to end.

## Details

1. **Mash** — steep crushed grain in hot water (typically 65-68°C) for ~60 minutes so enzymes convert starch to fermentable sugar.
2. **Lauter/Sparge** — separate the sweet liquid (wort) from the spent grain, rinsing the grain bed with hot water to recover remaining sugar.
3. **Boil** — boil the wort (usually 60-90 minutes), adding hops at scheduled times for bitterness, flavor, and aroma.
4. **Chill** — cool the wort quickly to pitching temperature to reduce infection risk and improve cold break.
5. **Ferment** — pitch yeast and hold at a style-appropriate temperature; primary fermentation usually takes 1-2 weeks.
6. **Package** — bottle with priming sugar or keg and force-carbonate.
