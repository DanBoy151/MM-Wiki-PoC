## Summary

The four core ingredient groups in beer, and what each one contributes.

## Details

**Fermentables** (malted grain, extract, sugar) provide the sugars yeast ferments into alcohol, plus color, body, and malt flavor. **Hops** provide bitterness, flavor, and aroma, and act as a mild preservative. **Yeast** converts sugar into alcohol and CO2, and contributes its own flavor/aroma character (esters, phenols) depending on strain. **Water** makes up 90%+ of the beer by volume, and its mineral profile affects both mash chemistry and final flavor/balance.

See a style's [[../../bjcp-styles/1-style-standard-american-beer/American-Lager|Characteristic Ingredients]] section (every BJCP style page has one) for what's typical for a given style.
