## Summary

What you actually need at each stage of brewing beer, from a first extract kit to all-grain.

## Details

**Extract/partial-mash starter kit**: a brew kettle (min. ~10L for partial-boil extract), a fermenter with airlock, a sanitizer (e.g. Star San), a hydrometer or refractometer, a long spoon, an auto-siphon, and bottles/caps or a keg setup.

**Moving to all-grain** adds: a mash tun (insulated cooler or dedicated vessel) with a false bottom or braid for lautering, a hot liquor tank or second kettle for sparge/strike water, and a larger boil kettle (~25-30L+ depending on batch size).

**Nice-to-haves as you go**: a wort chiller (immersion or plate) to speed cooling and reduce infection risk, a pH meter for water chemistry work, and temperature-controlled fermentation (even a swamp cooler helps).
