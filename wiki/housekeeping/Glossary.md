# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of terms used across this wiki. Link to a term with `[[Glossary#Term]]` instead of re-explaining it inline.

## OG (Original Gravity)
The density of the wort before fermentation, relative to water, e.g. `1.050`. Higher OG generally means more fermentable sugar and more potential alcohol.

## FG (Final Gravity)
The density of the beer after fermentation finishes. `OG` minus `FG` (roughly) tells you how much sugar the yeast consumed.

## ABV (Alcohol By Volume)
The percentage of the finished beer's volume that is alcohol, estimated from `OG` and `FG`.

## IBU (International Bitterness Units)
A measure of hop bitterness. Higher numbers taste more bitter, though perceived bitterness also depends on malt sweetness and body.

## SRM (Standard Reference Method)
A scale for beer color, from pale straw (2) to opaque black (40+).

## Attenuation
The percentage of sugar the yeast actually converted to alcohol and CO2. A yeast's "attenuation %" on a recipe page is typically its expected/average attenuation from the lab.

## Efficiency
For all-grain brewing: the percentage of the sugar available in the grain that actually ends up in the wort. Used to size a grain bill for a target `OG`.

## Pitching
Adding yeast to the wort to start fermentation.

## Krausen
The foamy head that forms on top of actively fermenting beer.

## Priming
Adding a small, calculated amount of sugar before bottling so the yeast produces carbonation in the sealed bottle.

## Off-Flavor
Any flavor or aroma that shouldn't be there for the intended style — see each beverage's `topics/Troubleshooting/` folder for common causes and fixes.

## BJCP
Beer Judge Certification Program — the organization whose style guidelines this wiki's `beer/bjcp-styles/` section is built from, and whose judges score entries at BJCP-sanctioned competitions.

## BeerXML
A standard file format for interchanging recipes between brewing software (BeerSmith, Brewer's Friend, etc.). See `Tools/beerxml-README.md` for this wiki's converter.
