# Ingredients
Beer has four core ingredients. Each has its own section.

| Ingredient | Role |
|---|---|
| [Malt](/beer/ingredients/malt) | Fermentable sugar, colour, body and flavour |
| [Water](/beer/ingredients/water) | About 90% of beer. Mineral content shapes flavour |
| [Hops](/beer/ingredients/hops) | Bitterness, flavour, aroma and preservation |
| [Yeast](/beer/ingredients/yeast) | Ferments sugar into alcohol and CO2, and creates much of the flavour |
