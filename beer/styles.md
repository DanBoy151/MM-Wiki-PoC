# Styles
A beer style is a group of beers that share characteristics such as strength, colour, ingredients and flavour. Styles are guidelines, not rules.

| Region | Known for |
|---|---|
| [Belgian](/beer/styles/belgian) | Expressive yeast, spices, high strength |
| [German](/beer/styles/german) | Clean lagers, wheat beers, malt focus |
| [Czech](/beer/styles/czech) | Pale lager, Saaz hops, soft water |
| [English](/beer/styles/english) | Cask ales, bitters, porters, stouts |

For competition style guides, see the BJCP guidelines and [Competition Brewing](/beer/techniques/competition-brewing).
