# Quick Start Guide
The fastest route to your first beer is a **beer kit** (a can of hopped malt extract with yeast) or a simple **extract recipe**. You will need about four weeks from brew day to glass.

## The Short Version
1. **Buy a starter kit.** See [Equipment](/beer/beginners/equipment).
2. **Pick a forgiving recipe.** A pale ale or amber ale using a kit or a [malt extract](/beer/ingredients/malt/malt-extract) recipe.
3. **Clean and sanitise everything** that touches the beer after the boil.
4. **Make the wort.** Dissolve the extract in hot water, boil with hops (if the recipe needs it) and top up with cold water.
5. **Cool to 18 - 24 C (64 - 75 F)** and pitch the yeast.
6. **Ferment for 10 - 14 days** at a steady 18 - 20 C (64 - 68 F).
7. **Bottle** with priming sugar. See [Bottle Conditioning](/beer/techniques/packaging/bottle-conditioning).
8. **Wait two weeks** at room temperature, then chill and enjoy.

## Timeline

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Brew day (2 - 4 hours) |
| Day 1 - 14 | Fermentation. Leave it alone. |
| Day 14 | Check gravity, bottle |
| Day 14 - 28 | Bottle conditioning |
| Day 28+ | Drink |
