# Gotchas
Mistakes almost everyone makes once.

## Sanitation
- **Skipping sanitiser on "just one thing".** Sample thieves, spoons, airlocks and hydrometers all need sanitising.
- **Scented cleaners.** Residue ends up in the beer.

## Brew Day
- **Boil-overs.** Stay at the kettle as the wort comes to a boil. Foam rises fast.
- **Adding extract on the burner.** It sinks and scorches. Turn off the heat first.
- **Squeezing the grain bag.** It extracts tannins and gives astringency.
- **Not chilling fast enough.** Leaving wort warm for hours invites infection and off flavours.

## Fermentation
- **Pitching too warm.** Always cool below 24 C (75 F) before adding yeast.
- **Fermenting too hot.** Fruity, solvent and "hot alcohol" flavours. A wet towel and a fan helps.
- **Judging by the airlock.** Bubbling is not a reliable indicator. Use a hydrometer.
- **Opening the lid to look.** Each peek risks infection and oxygen.

## Packaging
- **Bottling too early.** Causes gushers and bottle bombs. Confirm FG is stable for two days.
- **Wrong priming sugar amount.** Measure by weight, not volume.
- **Splashing at bottling.** Oxidation gives wet cardboard flavours.
- **Using twist-off or damaged bottles.** They may not hold pressure.

## Mindset
- **Impatience.** Beer improves with time. Give it at least 3 - 4 weeks.
- **Changing everything at once.** Change one variable per batch and keep a brew log.
- **Throwing out "bad" beer too soon.** Many off flavours fade with conditioning.
