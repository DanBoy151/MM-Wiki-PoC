# Brewing Process
This page gives the big picture. For exact quantities and steps, see [Brewing 101](/beer/Brewing-101).

## Stages

| # | Stage | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clean and sanitise** | Equipment is cleaned, then sanitised |
| 2 | **Mash or steep** | Grain is soaked in hot water to extract sugar. Extract brewers skip or shorten this. See [Mashing](/beer/techniques/mashing) |
| 3 | **Boil** | Wort is boiled, usually for 60 minutes, and [hops](/beer/ingredients/hops) are added |
| 4 | **Chill** | Wort is cooled quickly to yeast pitching temperature |
| 5 | **Pitch yeast** | [Yeast](/beer/ingredients/yeast) is added to the cooled wort |
| 6 | **Ferment** | Yeast converts sugar to alcohol and CO2. See [Fermentation](/beer/techniques/fermentation) |
| 7 | **Package** | Beer is bottled or kegged. See [Packaging](/beer/techniques/packaging) |
| 8 | **Condition** | Beer carbonates and flavours mellow |

## Key Numbers

| Measure | Meaning | Typical ale |
|---|---|---|
| Original gravity (OG) | Sugar before fermentation | 1.040 - 1.060 |
| Final gravity (FG) | Sugar left after fermentation | 1.008 - 1.016 |
| ABV | `(OG - FG) x 131.25` | 4 - 6% |
| Fermentation temperature | | 18 - 20 C (64 - 68 F) |

## Where Each Method Differs
Extract, partial mash and all grain only differ in stage 2. Everything after the boil is the same.
