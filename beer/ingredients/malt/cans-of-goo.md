# Cans of Goo
"Goo" is the affectionate name for the tinned, pre-hopped malt extract used in beer kits. A can typically holds 1.5 - 1.7 kg of concentrated hopped wort with a sachet of yeast on top.

## How They Work
1. Dissolve the contents in hot water.
2. Add sugar or extra malt extract (see below).
3. Top up with cold water to 21 - 23 L.
4. Pitch the yeast supplied (or a better one).

## Getting a Better Result
Kits can make decent beer if you avoid the shortcuts on the label.

| Tip | Why |
|---|---|
| Replace white sugar with dry malt extract or dextrose | Table sugar thins body and can give a cidery taste |
| Use a quality yeast instead of the kit sachet | Kit yeast is often old or poor quality |
| Ferment at 18 - 20 C | Warmer fermentation gives off flavours |
| Use fresh cans | Old extract darkens and tastes stale |
| Add hops (dry hop) | Kit hopping is often basic and dull |
| Condition for four weeks or more | Kits improve with age |
