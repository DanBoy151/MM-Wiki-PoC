# Hops
Hops are the flowers (cones) of *Humulus lupulus*. They balance malt sweetness with bitterness, add flavour and aroma and have antibacterial properties.

## How Hops Work
- **Alpha acids (AA)** become bitter iso-alpha acids only when boiled. Longer boils extract more bitterness.
- **Essential oils** provide flavour and aroma but evaporate with heat. Add late, or after the boil.

| When added | Bitterness | Flavour | Aroma |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60 min | High | None | None |
| 15 - 20 min | Medium | Some | Low |
| 0 - 5 min / flameout | Low | High | Medium |
| [Whirlpool](/beer/techniques/hopping/whirlpool-hops) | Low - medium | High | High |
| [Dry hop](/beer/techniques/hopping/dry-hops) | None | Medium | Very high |

## Forms

| Form | Notes |
|---|---|
| Pellets | Most common. Compact, store well, high utilisation |
| Whole leaf | Natural filter bed in kettle. Bulky, absorbs wort |
| Wet / fresh | Harvest-time only. Use roughly 5x the weight of dried |
| Cryo / lupulin powder | Concentrated resin and oils. Use about half the weight |

## Common Varieties by Origin

| Origin | Varieties | Character |
|---|---|---|
| USA | Cascade, Centennial, Citra, Mosaic, Simcoe | Citrus, pine, tropical fruit |
| England | East Kent Goldings, Fuggle, Challenger | Earthy, floral, woody |
| Germany | Hallertau, Tettnang, Hersbrucker, Magnum | Noble, spicy, floral |
| Czech | Saaz | Herbal, spicy, delicate |
| Australia / New Zealand | Galaxy, Nelson Sauvin, Motueka | Passionfruit, white wine, lime |

## Calculating Bitterness
IBU depends on weight, alpha acid %, boil time, wort gravity and volume. Use a brewing calculator (e.g. Tinseth formula). As a guide, 28 g (1 oz) of 6% AA hops boiled for 60 minutes in 19 L (5 gal) adds roughly 25 - 30 IBU.

## Storage
Hops oxidise quickly. Keep them sealed (vacuum or oxygen barrier bags), cold and dark - ideally in the freezer.
