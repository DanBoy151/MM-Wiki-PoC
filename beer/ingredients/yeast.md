# Yeast
Yeast is a single-celled fungus that eats sugar and produces alcohol, CO2 and flavour compounds (esters, phenols and others). Brewers "make wort; yeast makes beer".

## Organism Groups

| Group | Page | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| *Saccharomyces* | [Saccharomyces](/beer/ingredients/yeast/saccharomyces) | Ales and lagers |
| *Brettanomyces* | [Brett](/beer/ingredients/yeast/brett) | Funky and rustic beers |
| Bacteria | [Bugs](/beer/ingredients/yeast/bugs) | Sour beers |

## Forms

| Form | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Dry | Cheap, long shelf life, easy | Fewer strains |
| Liquid (pouch / vial) | Large strain range | Short life, often needs a [starter](/beer/techniques/fermentation/yeast-management/starters) |
| Slurry (harvested) | Free, healthy | Needs careful handling and hygiene |

## Four Rules for Healthy Yeast
1. **Pitch enough.** See [Yeast Management](/beer/techniques/fermentation/yeast-management).
2. **Oxygenate wort** before pitching.
3. **Control temperature.**
4. **Keep it clean.** Yeast is easily outcompeted by contamination.
