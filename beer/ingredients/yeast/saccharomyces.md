# Saccharomyces
The genus of "brewer's yeast". Nearly all beer is fermented with one of two species.

## Ale vs Lager

| | Ale (*S. cerevisiae*) | Lager (*S. pastorianus*) |
|---|---|---|
| Fermentation temp | 15 - 24 C (59 - 75 F) | 8 - 13 C (46 - 55 F) |
| Behaviour | Top-cropping / warm | Bottom-fermenting / cold |
| Flavour | Fruity esters, can be spicy | Clean, crisp |
| Time | 1 - 2 weeks | 3 - 8 weeks including lagering |
| Pitch rate | 0.75 million cells/mL/ degree Plato | 1.5 million cells/mL/ degree Plato |

## Common Strain Families

| Family | Example strains | Character |
|---|---|---|
| American | US-05, WLP001, Wyeast 1056 | Clean, neutral |
| English | S-04, WLP002, Wyeast 1968 | Fruity, malty, flocculent |
| Belgian | Abbaye, Saison, Witbier strains | Phenolic, spicy, estery |
| German wheat | WLP300, Wyeast 3068 | Banana and clove |
| Kveik | Voss, Hornindal | Very fast, tolerates 30 - 40 C |
| German lager | W-34/70, WLP830 | Clean, slight sulfur |

## Temperature Matters
Warmer fermentation increases esters (fruit) and fusel alcohols (hot, solventy). Staying near the bottom of a strain's range is the safest approach.

## Common Off-Flavours

| Off flavour | Cause |
|---|---|
| Diacetyl (butter) | Short fermentation, stressed yeast. Fix with a diacetyl rest |
| Acetaldehyde (green apple) | Beer taken off yeast too early |
| Fusel (hot alcohol) | Too warm, underpitched |
| Sulfur | Normal for lager yeast. Fades with conditioning |
