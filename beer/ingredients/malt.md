# Malt
Malt is cereal grain (usually barley) that has been soaked, allowed to start germinating and then dried in a kiln. This activates enzymes that convert starch to sugar when mashed. Kilning temperature sets colour and flavour.

## Forms of Malt

| Form | Page | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Hopped kit cans | [Cans of Goo](/beer/ingredients/malt/cans-of-goo) | First brews, minimal effort |
| Liquid and dry extract | [Malt Extract](/beer/ingredients/malt/malt-extract) | Extract and partial mash brewing |
| Whole malted grain | [Grain](/beer/ingredients/malt/grain) | Partial mash and all grain |

## Why Malt Matters
- **Fermentable sugar.** Typically 75 - 100% of the sugar in the beer.
- **Colour.** Pale straw through to black.
- **Flavour.** Bread, biscuit, caramel, toast, coffee and chocolate.
- **Body and head retention.** From proteins and unfermentable sugars.
