# Competition Brewing
Competitions provide structured feedback from trained judges and are a quick way to improve.

## Why Enter
- Honest, anonymous feedback on your beer.
- A benchmark against other homebrewers.
- Prizes, recognition and the chance to meet other brewers.

## How Judging Works
- Beers are judged blind against a style guide such as **BJCP**.
- Judges score aroma, appearance, flavour, mouthfeel and overall impression, out of 50.
- Entrants get a scoresheet with comments.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 45 - 50 | Outstanding |
| 38 - 44 | Excellent |
| 30 - 37 | Very good |
| 21 - 29 | Good |
| 14 - 20 | Fair |
| 0 - 13 | Problematic |

## Preparing an Entry
1. **Choose the right style.** Read the style guidelines carefully and brew to them.
2. **Enter a beer you've brewed before.** Tweak and repeat.
3. **Plan the timeline.** Brew and package in time for it to be at peak condition.
4. **Package cleanly.** Plain, clean bottles with no labels or marks (check rules), usually 2 x 330 - 500 mL.
5. **Check entry rules.** Number of bottles, shipping dates, fees and labelling.
6. **Fill in entry forms** accurately, including special ingredients and strength.

## Tips
- Fix the technical faults first (oxidation, infection, off flavours). Judges deduct heavily.
- Stay within the style's range.
- Carbonation and clarity matter.
- Use the feedback. Brew it again.
