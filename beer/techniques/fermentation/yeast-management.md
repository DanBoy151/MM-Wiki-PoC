# Yeast Management
Healthy yeast in the right quantity is the foundation of clean fermentation.

## Pitch Rates
The recommended starting point is cells per mL per degree Plato:

| Beer | Rate (million cells/mL/ degree Plato) |
|---|---|
| Ale | 0.75 |
| Lager | 1.5 |
| High-gravity ale (above 1.060) | 1.0 |

A 20 L batch at 1.050 (12.5 P) needs roughly 190 billion ale cells, about two packets of dry yeast or one liquid pack with a [starter](/beer/techniques/fermentation/yeast-management/starters).

## Sources

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Dry yeast | Rehydrate in sterile water at 30 - 35 C for 15 minutes. Sprinkling directly is also acceptable |
| Liquid yeast | Cell count drops by about 20% per month. Make a starter if old or for stronger beer |
| Harvested slurry | Free. Use within 1 - 2 weeks for best quality |

## Harvesting and Repitching
1. Collect yeast from a healthy, clean fermentation (avoid very hoppy, very high gravity).
2. Store in a sanitised jar in the fridge, under beer or cooled boiled water.
3. Repitch within 2 weeks. Yeast viability falls with time.
4. Limit to 5 - 10 generations before going back to a fresh pack.

## Signs of Unhealthy Yeast
- Long lag time (more than 24 hours).
- Stalled fermentation or high FG.
- Excessive sulfur, diacetyl, or fusel flavours.

## Supporting Yeast
- **Oxygenate** wort. Shake, stir or use pure oxygen.
- **Add yeast nutrient** for high gravity or adjunct-heavy worts.
- **Control temperature** to avoid stress.
