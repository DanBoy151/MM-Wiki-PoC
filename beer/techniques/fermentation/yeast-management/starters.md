# Starters
A starter is a small batch of low-gravity wort used to grow yeast before pitching into your main batch.

## When You Need One
- Liquid yeast older than a month.
- Beers above about 1.060, or any lager.
- Washing and repitching yeast of uncertain viability.

## Basic Recipe
- **100 g DME per 1 L water** (about 1.037 gravity).
- Boil 10 minutes, cool and transfer to a sanitised flask or jar. A drop of yeast nutrient helps.
- Add yeast and aerate. Cover with foil or an airlock.

## Sizing

| Batch | Yeast pack | Starter size |
|---|---|---|
| 20 L ale, 1.050 | Fresh liquid | 1 L (optional) |
| 20 L ale, 1.065 | Fresh liquid | 2 L |
| 20 L lager, 1.050 | Fresh liquid | 3 - 4 L or step up |

Use an online yeast calculator for exact figures.

## Stir Plate vs Still
- **Stir plate:** constant stirring boosts oxygenation and growth. Roughly doubles yield.
- **Still starter:** shake occasionally. Needs a larger volume.

## Process
1. Make wort and cool.
2. Pitch yeast and aerate.
3. Ferment at room temperature for 24 - 48 hours.
4. Optionally **cold crash** and decant the spent liquid to avoid adding it to beer.
5. Pitch the yeast slurry into the batch at fermentation temperature.
