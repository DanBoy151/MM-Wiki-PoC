# Canning
Cans block light completely, seal more reliably than caps and are lightweight and portable. A good option for hoppy beer where oxygen and light are enemies.

## Equipment
- Manual or electric can seamer
- Cans and lids (typically 355 mL, 473 mL or 500 mL)
- Beer gun, or counter-pressure filler
- Keg of carbonated beer
- CO2 for purging
- Sanitiser, rinse bottle

## Process
1. Carbonate the beer in a keg.
2. Chill cans, filler and beer to as cold as possible.
3. Sanitise cans and lids.
4. **Purge the can with CO2.**
5. Fill slowly via the filler to avoid foaming.
6. Top with a little foam, push on the lid and **seam immediately**.
7. Rinse the can and store cold.

## Seam Quality
A poorly formed seam leaks and lets oxygen in. Check seams by cutting open a sample and measuring with a micrometer or seam gauge. Aim for correct seam thickness and a hook overlap of at least 1.0 mm. Seam gauges come with the seamer instructions.

## Notes
- **Oxygen.** The main enemy. Purge, fill under CO2 and keep cold.
- **Cans can't be bottle conditioned** in typical lined cans; carbonate in a keg first.
- **Fill time and quality** improve with a counter-pressure filler.
- **Label** the beer name, date and style.
