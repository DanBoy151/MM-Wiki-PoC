# Dip Hopping
Dip hopping is a short, hot steep of hops in a mesh bag, "dipped" into wort or hot water for a short time and then removed. The goal is to extract aroma and flavour oils while keeping extra bitterness low.

## Method
1. Put hops in a sanitised mesh or muslin bag.
2. Dip the bag in wort, either as it leaves the kettle or in a portion of hot wort, at 70 - 80 C (158 - 176 F).
3. Agitate for 1 - 10 minutes, then lift out and let drain.
4. Optionally add the infused liquid back to the fermenter.

## Compared to Other Methods

| Method | Temperature | Bitterness | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dip hopping | 70 - 80 C, brief | Low | Medium |
| [Whirlpool](/beer/techniques/hopping/whirlpool-hops) | 70 - 85 C, 15 - 30 min | Low - medium | Low |
| [Dry hopping](/beer/techniques/hopping/dry-hops) | 15 - 20 C, days | None | Low |

Terminology varies between breweries. Some use "dip hopping" for specific short additions at the end of a whirlpool.
