# Whirlpool Hops
Hops are added after the boil has finished and held in hot wort, before chilling. Also called a "hop stand". The wort is hot enough to extract oils and some alpha acids but cool enough to retain volatile aroma.

## Guidelines

| Parameter | Typical |
|---|---|
| Temperature | 70 - 85 C (158 - 185 F) |
| Time | 15 - 30 minutes |
| Rate | 2 - 6 g/L (more for hazy IPAs) |

## Temperature and Bitterness
Isomerisation continues above about 80 C (176 F), adding bitterness. Many brewers cool to 75 C (167 F) first to get flavour with a soft bitterness. At full flameout temperature, use a modest hop quantity and reduce 60 minute additions to balance IBU.

## Steps
1. End of boil: turn off the heat, stir to create a vortex.
2. Cool to the target temperature (optional).
3. Add hops. Cover and stand 15 - 30 minutes.
4. Chill and transfer.

## Why It Works
Compared to a boil addition at 0 minutes, the lower temperature preserves more oils. Compared to dry hopping, less hop vegetal matter is carried over and there's less risk of oxygen pickup.
