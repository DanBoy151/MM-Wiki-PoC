# Hopping
Where and when hops are added decides what they give the beer. See [Hops](/beer/ingredients/hops) for the ingredient itself.

| Technique | Stage | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| Boil additions | Kettle, 60 - 0 min | Bitterness, some flavour |
| [Whirlpool Hops](/beer/techniques/hopping/whirlpool-hops) | After boil, before chilling | Flavour and aroma, soft bitterness |
| [Dip Hopping](/beer/techniques/hopping/dip-hopping) | Brief steep in hot wort | Aroma with minimal bitterness |
| [Dry Hops](/beer/techniques/hopping/dry-hops) | Fermenter or keg | Strong aroma, no bitterness |

## Planning a Hop Schedule
1. Pick total IBU for the style.
2. Add a bittering charge for roughly 50 - 60% of IBU.
3. Use late and whirlpool additions for flavour.
4. Finish with a dry hop if the style calls for aroma.
