# Fermentation
Fermentation is where wort becomes beer. Good fermentation practice makes more difference to quality than almost any other change.

## Phases

| Phase | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Lag | 0 - 12 h | Yeast adapts, multiplies, uses oxygen |
| Primary / active | 1 - 5 days | Rapid sugar consumption, krausen, most flavour |
| Maturation | 3 - 14 days | Yeast cleans up diacetyl and acetaldehyde |
| Conditioning | Days - weeks | Clearing, flavour rounding, carbonation |

## The Big Four Controls
1. **Pitch rate** - [Yeast Management](/beer/techniques/fermentation/yeast-management)
2. **Oxygen** - 8 - 10 ppm in cooled wort for ales. None afterwards.
3. **Temperature** - hold steady, 18 - 20 C for most ales, 10 - 12 C for lagers.
4. **Time** - wait for terminal gravity, then a few days more.
