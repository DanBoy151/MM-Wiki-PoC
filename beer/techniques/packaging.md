# Packaging
Packaging carbonates and preserves the beer. The main enemy is **oxygen**.

| Method | Page | Cost | Effort | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bottle conditioning | [Bottle Conditioning](/beer/techniques/packaging/bottle-conditioning) | Low | Medium | Beginners, long storage |
| Kegging | [Kegging](/beer/techniques/packaging/kegging) | High up front | Low once set up | Regular brewers |
| Canning | [Canning](/beer/techniques/packaging/canning) | Medium | Medium | Portability, hop-forward beer |
| Pasteurisation | [Pasteurisation](/beer/techniques/packaging/pasteurisation) | Low | Medium | Sweet or stabilised beer |

## Carbonation Levels

| Style | Volumes of CO2 |
|---|---|
| British ales, stouts | 1.5 - 2.0 |
| Most ales, lagers | 2.2 - 2.7 |
| Wheat beers, Belgian ales | 2.8 - 4.0 |
