# Czech Styles
The Czech Republic gave the world Pilsner in 1842. Czech lagers are known for their soft maltiness, floral hop bite and exceptional drinkability.

## Common Styles

| Style | OG | ABV | IBU | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Czech Pale Lager (světlý) | 1.028 - 1.044 | 3 - 4.1% | 20 - 35 | Light, refreshing |
| Czech Premium Pale Lager | 1.044 - 1.060 | 4.2 - 5.8% | 30 - 45 | The classic Pilsner |
| Czech Amber Lager | 1.044 - 1.060 | 4.4 - 5.8% | 20 - 35 | Caramel, bready |
| Czech Dark Lager | 1.044 - 1.060 | 4.4 - 5.8% | 18 - 34 | Roasty, smooth |

## Key Ingredients
- **Bohemian Pilsner malt**, a lightly kilned, floral base.
- **Saaz hops**: herbal, spicy and floral.
- **Very soft water** with low minerals. See [Water](/beer/ingredients/water).
- **Czech lager yeast**, giving a slight diacetyl note that is acceptable in this style.

## Technique
- Traditional [double decoction](/beer/techniques/mashing/decoction) for a rich malt character.
- Cool fermentation at 8 - 12 C, then lagering for 6 - 8 weeks or more.
- Hop with a late Saaz addition for aroma.
